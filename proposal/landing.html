Frequently asked questions

Common questions about RelayGuard's webhook reliability and recovery architecture.

How does RelayGuard track webhook attempts?
RelayGuard acts as an intelligent relay proxy. Every HTTP dispatch records the exact timestamp, response code, latency, and response headers for complete operational visibility.
What happens when an endpoint returns 5xx or times out?
Transient failures trigger our configurable exponential backoff algorithm with jitter to prevent thunderous herd problems on your receiving microservices.
How does failure diagnosis differentiate facts from hypotheses?
Observed facts (like HTTP 401 response codes and server latency) are displayed as verified telemetry. AI-assisted hypotheses are explicitly flagged as interpretations accompanied by a confidence rating.
Can I safely replay events without duplicate charges?
Yes. RelayGuard supports controlled replays with clear side-effect warnings and maintains original Event IDs to preserve idempotency safeguards in receiving systems.
